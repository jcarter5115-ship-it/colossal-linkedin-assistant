# Privacy Policy
**Colossal LinkedIn Engagement Assistant**

Effective Date: October 30, 2026

## 1. Overview

The Colossal LinkedIn Engagement Assistant is a personal productivity application designed to help its operator prepare and manage engagement with LinkedIn posts.

## 2. Information Collected

The application may process the following information when authorized:

- LinkedIn member profile information.
- LinkedIn post URLs and publicly visible post content supplied to the application.
- Text generated for comments and repost captions.
- LinkedIn authorization tokens required for approved API operations.

## 3. Use of Information

Information is used solely to:

- Identify and organize relevant LinkedIn posts.
- Generate personalized engagement drafts.
- Support authorized LinkedIn publishing operations.
- Prevent duplicate engagement.

## 4. Information Sharing

Personal information is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising purposes.

Information may be processed by service providers strictly as necessary to operate the application.

## 5. Data Security and Retention

Reasonable safeguards will be used to protect application data and authorization credentials.

LinkedIn credentials will never be requested directly or stored in publicly accessible source code.

## 6. Third-Party Services

This application integrates with LinkedIn subject to LinkedIn's applicable terms, developer policies, and API permissions.

LinkedIn's privacy policy is available at https://www.linkedin.com/legal/privacy-policy.

## 7. User Control

The application operator may discontinue use of the application and revoke its LinkedIn authorization through LinkedIn's account settings.

## 8. Contact

For privacy questions or requests concerning this application, contact the application operator through the contact information associated with the application.

## 9. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated as application functionality changes.
